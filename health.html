Health Notice

Effective date: 18 October 2026

1. Information, not treatment

Xolot shows general information about nutrition, supplements and training. It is not a medical device and does not replace advice from a doctor, dietitian or pharmacist.

2. Before you start supplements

Ask a doctor or pharmacist first if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, take medication, have a chronic condition, are planning surgery or are under 18. If you mark a health condition in the app, it stops giving timing advice for supplements and asks you to check with your doctor.

Upper limits and safety notes come from NIH Office of Dietary Supplements and EFSA. They are general values for healthy adults and may not fit you.

3. Targets and estimates

Calorie and macro targets are calculated with published formulas (for example Mifflin-St Jeor). Calories burned are approximate values from the Compendium of Physical Activities and are shown only as estimates.

4. Training

Start slowly and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath. Ask a doctor before starting a new training plan if you have a heart condition or other health problem.

5. Emergencies

In an emergency call 112 or your local emergency number. Do not wait for information from an app.
