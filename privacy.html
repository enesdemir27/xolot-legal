Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

1. Who we are

Xolot is developed and published by Enes Demir, Türkiye ("we", "us"). We are the controller of personal data processed by the app within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

Contact: demirenes2772@gmail.com

2. The short version

Xolot works without an account and without servers of our own. What you enter stays on your device. We do not see it, we do not sell data, and the app contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

3. Data you enter and where it is kept

Profile details (optional first name, optional birth date, age, sex, height, weight, body fat, goal, activity level, diet, health conditions you choose to mark), meals, water, supplements and their times, workouts, weigh-ins, step counts, your look and other settings.

All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device (a local database and app preferences). It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, under Google's terms. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.

If something in the app fails, the error and basic device details (Android version, app version) are kept on your device only. They are sent to us only if you share them yourself (Profile → About → Error report).

4. Health information

Some of the data, such as weight, health conditions and supplements, is health data. It is processed only on your device to calculate targets and show information to you, on the basis of your explicit consent when you enter it (GDPR Art. 9(2)(a), KVKK Art. 6). You can change or delete it at any time.

5. Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you scan a supplement label. The text is recognised on your device with Google ML Kit. The app does not store or send the photo. When you pick a photo, the system photo picker gives the app access to that one image only.

6. Steps and activity

With your permission the app reads step counts from your phone's step sensor and from the Google Play services Recording API, which records steps on the device and needs no Google account. Daily totals are stored in the app on your device.

7. Reminders and invites

If you turn on supplement reminders, the app asks for the notification permission and schedules your supplement times with Android's own notification system on your device. Reminder texts are created on the device and are not sent anywhere.

If you installed the app from an invite link, the invite code is read once on your device from Google Play's install source (Install Referrer) and is stored only on your device. Invite and thank-you codes are never sent to a server; whether you share a code is up to you.

8. Services from Google that the app uses

Google Play billing. If you subscribe to Xolot Premium, Google Play handles the payment and the subscription under the Google Privacy Policy. The app asks Google Play on your device whether a subscription is active. We do not receive your payment details.

Google ML Kit (on-device text recognition). According to Google, ML Kit may send diagnostic and usage information such as device model, operating system version, app information and performance metrics to Google to keep the service working. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit and not shared with third parties. Your label photos and their text are not sent.

Google Play services (Recording API for steps) works on your device.

Google Play in-app review: the app may now and then ask you to rate it. Google Play receives your rating and review; no personal data reaches us.

9. Sharing and links

When you share a card or a weekly recap, the picture and text go to the app you choose, under that app's terms. Links to these pages, the Play Store or email open in other apps.

10. What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no selling or sharing of personal information, no profiling for marketing and no account.

11. Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it: Profile → Data → Delete all data, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. You can cancel a subscription at any time in Google Play; Google keeps purchase records according to its own policies.

12. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data, to object, to withdraw consent and to complain to a data protection authority. Under KVKK Art. 11 you have similar rights. California residents: we do not sell or share personal information. Because we hold no data on servers, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app. For any question, write to us.

13. Children

Xolot is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly process data of children.

14. Transfers abroad

Data leaves your device only through the Google services named above, which may process it outside your country under Google's safeguards.

15. Security

App data lives in storage that Android keeps private to the app. Protect your phone with a screen lock.

16. Changes

If this policy changes we update this page and its date. Important changes are also mentioned in the app or in the store listing.
